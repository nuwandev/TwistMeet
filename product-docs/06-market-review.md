# Market and source review

Checked 4 October 2026. Public product descriptions are evidence of advertised capabilities, not independent usability tests. Re-check products before making a build/buy decision.

## Existing options that materially overlap

| Product | What public sources say | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| RecordRanks / Cubing Contests | Competition and sports-community management, competition setup, results/rankings, competitor management, historical data and API; Cubing Contests describes live result entry and community administration. | Strong overlap with organizer, results and history requirements. Direct competitor for general competition management. Test actual day-of-event and beginner workflows before claiming a gap. |
| Cubing.co | Advertises offline tournaments, live tracking, smart cubes, private tournaments, qualification stages, settings, history and reports. | Strong overlap with tournament hosting and smart-cube path. Validate what it does not cover in the target club setting. |
| WCA Live and WCA tools | Official competition result administration and WCA rules/scramble workflows. | Relevant benchmark and integration/reference ecosystem; not a substitute for an unofficial club product, and app cannot confer WCA sanction. |
| ScrambleCube, Zkt Timer and cube timers | Existing tools cover timer, scramble, 3D visualization, shared solving or smart-cube features in varying combinations. | A 3D cube or timer alone is not a unique product wedge. Confirm current feature details and licensing before reuse. |
| Spreadsheet + physical timer | Low setup cost, familiar, works offline, flexible. | The real competitor for small casual events; the product must be easier and more reliable than a sheet, not merely more feature-rich. |

## Differentiation hypothesis

Potential wedge to test: a guided, mobile-first event-day command center for first-time and small-club organizers that can coordinate guest entry, judge-driven physical timing, exceptions, prepared scramble guidance and public display without requiring every competitor to create an account. This remains a hypothesis. RecordRanks and Cubing.co may already satisfy much of it, so hands-on research is a prerequisite.

Potential moat, if validated: high trust in event execution, easy recurring organizer workflow, reliable offline/recovery behavior, accessible novice instructions and integrations that let clubs retain existing cuber identities/results. “More features” and animated rankings are not a moat by themselves.

## Important correction to prior framing

Earlier conversational notes treated RecordRanks as if all configurable advancement formats were established current features and framed a specific combination as a clear market gap. Public sources checked for this product pack show that RecordRanks has substantial competition administration and history capabilities; its public Cubing Contests page even lists tournament format as a prioritized future feature. Cubing.co advertises capabilities close to the proposed offline tournament flow. Therefore this specification does not declare the concept unique or the market gap proven.
